# Task 1: Top Cuisines

## Objective

Identify the top three most common cuisines and calculate the percentage of restaurants serving each cuisine.

## Results

| Cuisine | Restaurant Count | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| North Indian | 3,960 | 41.46% |
| Chinese | 2,735 | 28.64% |
| Fast Food | 1,986 | 20.79% |

## Key Insight

North Indian is the most commonly served cuisine in the dataset, followed by Chinese and Fast Food.

# Task 3: Price Range Distribution

## Objective

Analyze the distribution of restaurants across different price ranges and calculate the percentage of restaurants in each range.

## Results

| Price Range | Restaurant Count | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 4,444 | 46.53% |
| 2 | 3,113 | 32.59% |
| 3 | 1,408 | 14.74% |
| 4 | 586 | 6.14% |

## Key Insight

Most restaurants fall within Price Range 1 and Price Range 2, while Price Range 4 has the lowest number of restaurants.
